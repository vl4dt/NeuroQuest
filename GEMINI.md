# Project: NeuroQuest - Flashcards Evolved

## Features
- Beautiful UI that feels natural across Android, iOS, desktop and web.
- Learning Engine
  - Adaptive SRS: Starts with SM-2 logic but evolves with your personal recall speed and confidence ratings.
  - Multi-phase mastery:
    - Recognition (see the answer in options)
    - Recall (type or say it from memory)
    - Application (real-world mini scenarios or problem solving)
  - Mode switch: If the system detects you’re breezing through, it upgrades the challenge automatically.

## Core Philosophy
- Memory science first → True spaced repetition based on proven algorithms (SM-2 variant, adaptive fine-tuning per user).
- Fun and immersion → Game mechanics make it feel like you’re progressing in a story or quest.
- Effortless creation → AI turns your notes, documents, or videos into optimized cards instantly.
- Social + quality → Peer-reviewed decks, team learning, and live study battles.

## Game Layer:
- XP, levels, and skill trees: Each topic or deck has its own “skill tree” you fill in.
- Daily quests: Study goals disguised as missions (“Defeat the 10-card boss of Spanish Verbs”).
- Streak rewards: Cosmetic unlocks (card back styles, sound effects, avatars).
- Boss challenges: After mastering a set, you face a timed recall round with “boss” difficulty questions.

## AI-Powered Card Creation
- Text to flashcards: Paste a PDF, article, or lecture transcript → system auto-generates well-structured cards.
- Video to flashcards: Upload a video or YouTube link → AI pulls key facts and context as cards.
- Auto-Hint Engine: If you miss a card, AI offers progressively stronger hints without showing the answer.

## Social + Collaboration
- Deck Co-Authoring: Multiple people can edit, annotate, and version control a deck.
- Peer Review Scores: Decks have quality ratings by subject experts or high-performing learners.
- Live Study Battles: Multiplayer quiz matches with SRS-based questions—your wins push your deck mastery too.
- Study Groups: Share decks, track group progress, and assign “learning roles” (e.g., explainer, challenger).

## Smart Engagement
- Emotion-aware pacing: Detects if you’re rushing, guessing, or slowing down; adapts difficulty or injects fun breaks.
- Contextual learning: Pulls in related cards when you miss a concept, so you strengthen the foundation before moving on.
- Micro-learning injection: Sends 1–3 high-priority cards into idle moments (e.g., phone lock screen, smartwatch notification).

## Cross-Integration
- Import from anywhere: Anki, Quizlet, Brainscape, CSV, Notion, Obsidian.
- Export decks in multiple formats for total portability.
- Calendar & Habit Tracker sync: Learning is built into your daily flow.
- API & SDK: Third-party apps can create, update, and schedule cards.
